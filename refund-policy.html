Nature of the Product

SketchUpGurus AI operates on a pay-as-you-go model where users purchase digital rendering credits (e.g., Starter, Pro, or Studio packs). These credits are non-tangible, irrevocable digital goods that grant access to our cloud-based AI rendering engine.

02

No Refunds for Render Quality

Artificial Intelligence is an automated and highly creative process. While we provide strict geometry locks and material overrides to guide the AI, we cannot guarantee that every generated image will perfectly match your subjective expectations or physical real-world physics.

We do not offer refunds for credits consumed on renders that you simply do not like or that contain AI-generated artifacts.

We strongly encourage all users to purchase the smallest package ($5 Starter Pack) to test the plugin's capabilities and ensure it meets their workflow needs before purchasing larger credit packs.
