Delivery Timeframe

Instant Delivery

Upon successful completion of your payment through our secure gateway (Razorpay), your digital rendering credits are delivered instantly and automatically.

The processing time is typically immediate — your credit balance inside the SketchUp plugin will update within seconds of a successful transaction.

03

How Delivery Works

  • 1
    Authenticate
    You authenticate your account inside the SketchUpGurus AI plugin using your email address.
  • 2
    Select & Pay
    You select a credit package and complete the payment via Razorpay's secure gateway.
  • 3
    Instant Credit
    Our servers instantly verify the payment and attach the purchased credits to your authenticated email address.
  • 4
    Balance Refreshed
    The plugin UI automatically refreshes to display your new available credit balance. You're ready to render!

Delivery Issues and Delays

While our automated delivery system is designed to be instant, occasional internet connectivity issues or banking delays can occur.

⚠️
If your bank confirms that your account was charged, but your credit balance does not update within 5 minutes, please try restarting the SketchUp application first.

If credits still do not appear after a restart, this is considered a "failed delivery." Please contact our support team immediately at sg@sketchupgurus.co.in with your Razorpay payment receipt or transaction ID.

⏱ Resolution Time We will manually verify the payment and credit your account within 24 hours of receiving your support request.
