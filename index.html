Process

Three Steps.
Zero Complexity.

SketchUpGurus AI plugs directly into your existing SketchUp workflow. No new tools to learn, no complex node editors — just results.

01
Install the Plugin

One-click install directly inside SketchUp Extension Warehouse. Your model geometry, materials, and saved camera views are preserved automatically.

02
Set Style & Mood

Choose your render style, time of day, weather, and lighting mood from our presets — or type exactly what you want. Our AI interprets and applies it instantly.

03
Export & Present

Photorealistic renders export in under 60 seconds at up to 8K resolution. Share directly with clients or download for your presentation deck.

Meet the Creator

Suraj Kumar

Architect × AI Builder
Bridging Architecture & AI

Working directly in the architecture field, I experienced firsthand the constant friction between designing a beautiful 3D model and making it truly photorealistic. Traditional rendering tools often require hours of tweaking lighting, adjusting materials, and waiting for heavy renders.

I knew there had to be a faster way to visualize designs at the speed of thought. That's how SketchUpGurus AI was born.

My mission was simple — build a tool that works directly inside SketchUp and uses AI to transform raw viewports into stunning, photorealistic renders in seconds. No complex nodes, no heavy hardware, no waiting.

Today, SketchUpGurus AI is more than just a plugin. It's a growing community of architects, designers, and creators embracing the future of AI-powered design. Whether you're designing interiors or large-scale projects, SketchUpGurus helps you focus on what matters most — creating incredible spaces.
